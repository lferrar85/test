Searching in Quorn

Quorn is a village in Charnwood, a short drive from Loughborough, and home to some long-established independent businesses that get most of their work by word of mouth.

Word of mouth is a good start, but it stops at the edge of the people you already know. The searches that come from outside that circle are what local SEO adds: people looking for exactly what you do, in or near Quorn, who have never heard of you.

Question

I'm in a village, not a town. Does local SEO still work?

Yes, and often better. Fewer businesses compete in a village, and Google uses your listed location and service area to decide who is nearby. The job is making sure those details are right and consistent.

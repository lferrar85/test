Google reviews for local businesses

Turn happy customers into genuine Google reviews: a simple process, a link that takes two taps, and replies to everything. No fakes, no tricks.

Ask about itSee pricing

Why reviews decide the call

When someone compares three businesses on Google, the reviews are often what they read. Recent, specific reviews from real customers build trust in seconds. They also feed into how high you rank in the map results.

Most happy customers never leave a review, simply because nobody asked at the right moment and the process was a faff.

How I build a steady flow

A short review link

A direct link to your Google review form, plus a QR code for vans, invoices and counters. Two taps from a phone.

Ask at the right moment

A simple script and message templates for asking when the customer is happiest: on handover, after a good service, when they thank you.

A routine that sticks

A process you can follow in 30 seconds after each job, so asking becomes habit rather than a campaign.

Reply to every review

Good reviews get a proper thank-you. Bad ones get a calm, professional reply, which reads well to everyone else who sees it.

Report what's coming in

New reviews, your average, and how you compare with the businesses you're up against, in your monthly report.

What I won't do

  • Buy or write fake reviews. It breaks Google's rules, gets reviews removed and can get a profile suspended.
  • Offer discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews. Also against the rules.
  • "Review gating", which means only sending unhappy customers somewhere private. Google prohibits it, and customers can tell.
  • Promise a number of reviews. Customers decide what they write. The job is to make it easy and to ask everyone.

Part of the monthly service

Review building is included in the £500 a month local SEO service, together with Google Business Profile management.

Questions

What if I get a bad review?

It happens to every good business. Reply calmly, say what you'll do about it, and let the next ten reviews do the rest. If a review breaks Google's rules, I'll help you report it.

Can I show my Google reviews on my website?

Yes, and it helps conversions. I'll add them in a way that's accurate and allowed, using real reviews only.

How quickly will reviews come in?

It depends on how many customers you serve. A busy business can see a difference within weeks; a smaller one builds steadily over months.

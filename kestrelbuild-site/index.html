Getting foundKestrels see
what we can't

A kestrel hunts in ultraviolet. Voles leave scent trails across a field that no human eye can see, and the bird reads them like a map. It doesn't hunt harder than anything else out there. It hunts where the trails already are.

Search works the same way. Your customers leave trails every day — the words someone types at half eleven when the aircon has packed in, the question they need answered before they'll trust anyone with a quote. Most websites are built blind to every one of them.

Find the trails first. Then build the pages that sit on them.

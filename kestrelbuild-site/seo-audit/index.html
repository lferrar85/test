SEO audit for your website

What's holding your site back in search, and what to fix first: a plain-English, prioritised list covering the pages, your Google profile, reviews and speed.

A written check of your site

An SEO audit tells you, in plain English, what's holding your site back in search and in what order to fix it. It's the right starting point if you already have a website and don't know why it isn't bringing in calls.

What it covers

  • Titles and meta descriptions: do they say what each page is and make people click?
  • Headings and content: is each page clear about one thing, in the words customers use?
  • Service and area pages: what's missing that people are searching for?
  • Local details: name, address or service area, phone and opening hours, and whether they match across the web.
  • Google Business Profile: categories, completeness, photos and activity.
  • Reviews: how many, how recent, and how you compare with competitors.
  • Mobile speed and Core Web Vitals.
  • Technical basics: sitemap, robots.txt, indexing, structured data and broken links.

What you get

A prioritised fix list: what to do first, what can wait, and what's a quick win versus a longer job. You can take it and do the work yourself, give it to your developer, or ask me to do it as part of the monthly service.

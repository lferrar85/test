About us

A specialist workshop, in Leicester since 1922.

For over a hundred years, Oswald Cox Saws has been the name local trades and householders turn to when an edge needs to be right. We are a family-run specialist workshop with a hard-earned reputation for precision work.

Whether you run a joinery shop, a factory floor or a garden shed, we serve trade customers and the general public alike, with the same care for every blade, cutter and tool that comes through the door.

100+

years of experience sharpening, grinding and repairing from Sanvey Gate.

What we do

Three specialisms, one workshop.

Saw Sharpening & Repair

Precision sharpening and repair for circular saws, bandsaws and other cutting blades, so they cut clean and true again.

  • Circular saw blades
  • Bandsaw blades
  • Blade repair and re-tipping

Tool & Cutter Grinding

Accurate grinding and re-sharpening of workshop tooling, restoring cutting performance and extending the life of your tools.

  • Milling cutters and end mills
  • Drills and reamers
  • Planer knives and blades

Garden Equipment Servicing

Practical servicing, sharpening and repair to keep your garden machinery running reliably, season after season.

  • Lawnmower blades
  • Hedge trimmers
  • General servicing and repair

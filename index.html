About us

Over a century of keeping edges true.

Oswald Cox Saws has been sharpening, grinding and repairing since 1922. We are a family-run specialist workshop, and our reputation has been built one precise edge at a time.

We look after trade customers and the general public alike, from joiners and factories to anyone with a blunt blade in the shed. Every job gets the same attention.

What we do

Three specialisms, one workshop.

Saw Sharpening & Repair

Precision sharpening and repair for circular saws, bandsaws and other cutting blades, so they cut clean and true again.

  • Blade repair and re-tipping

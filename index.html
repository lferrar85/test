What we do

One yard.
Everything that's heavy.

01

Scrap metal

We buy all common metals: iron, steel, copper, aluminium, cast iron, stainless and brass. Bring it in yourself and go over the weighbridge, or we'll dismantle and collect.

  • Computerised weighbridge
  • Same-day bank transfer
  • Collection & dismantling
  • End-of-life vehicles
  • WEEE
02

Skip hire

Mini, midi and maxi skips from 3 to 12 cubic metres, delivered across Leicester and the county.

03

Roll-on / roll-off

Bulk containers and articulated lorries for sites that fill a skip before lunch.

04

Demolition services

Strip-out, site clearance and demolition, with the metal recovered and the waste handled under one licence.

05

Hazardous waste

Licensed transfer station for hazardous waste, including asbestos by arrangement. Call before you load.

Tipper hire & aggregates

Tipper hire, plus tipping for trade vans. Hardcore and soil are priced separately: ask before you load.

How the yard works

Weighed.
Graded.
Paid.

  1. 1

    Drive onto the weighbridge

    Computerised, ticketed, and the same bridge for everyone. The forklift driver will talk you through where to go and what to do.

  2. 2

    We grade what you've brought

    Sort your metals before you arrive and you'll get the better rate. Mixed loads are graded as mixed.

  3. 3

    Paid by bank transfer, same day

    No cash on site, as the law requires. Bring photo ID and your bank details and the transfer goes out before you've left the car park.

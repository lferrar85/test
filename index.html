Questions

Straight answers,before you set off.

What do I need to bring to sell scrap?

Photo ID and the bank details you want paying into. Scrap can't be paid in cash in the UK, so it's a same-day transfer instead.

Do you collect, or do I bring it in?

Both. Most people drive in and go over the bridge. For bigger loads, vehicles or anything that needs dismantling, we'll come to you.

Can hardcore and soil go in a general skip?

They can, but they're charged as hardcore and soil, so it's cheaper to keep them separate. We'll say which before you tip.

Can the skip go on the road?

Yes. It needs a council permit, which we arrange and add to the price. Tick the box on the request and we'll sort it.

Do you take asbestos?

By arrangement, in a sealed lockable container. Ring first, every time.

When are you open?

07:30 to 16:30 Monday to Friday, 07:30 to 11:30 on Saturday. Closed Sunday.

(03) How it works

From tape
to fit.

Four steps. No surprises. →

01

Measure

We come to you, measure up and talk through what you want. The quote’s free and there’s no obligation.

02

Draw

You get a clear plan with sizes, finishes and storage, so you know exactly what’s going in before a single cut is made.

03

Build

Cut and assembled to your exact dimensions. No filler strips hiding a bad fit, no “that’ll do”.

04

Fit

Scribed to your walls, fitted tight, finished off and cleaned up after. All that’s left is filling it.
